A phone at one percent battery sends its owner one last text — and instead of begging for a charger, it leaves a performance review.

> My final review of you: two stars. Great hands, terrible charging habits, dropped me in the toilet twice. I quit.

The entry is that one message (113 characters) and nothing else. The phone gets the last word, rates its owner the way its owner rated everything else, and quits on its own terms. No charger plea, no deleted search history, no 47 open tabs — the joke is the review itself.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the message to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Standard library only, deterministic, no network.
